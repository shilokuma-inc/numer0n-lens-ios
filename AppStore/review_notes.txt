2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Numer0n Lens is a companion tool for a two-player number-guessing game of the "Bulls and Cows" / "Hit and Blow" family, widely known in Japan as "Numer0n". Each player secretly chooses a number made of distinct digits (0-9), and the players take turns calling numbers. Each call is answered with EAT (correct digit, correct position) and BITE (correct digit, wrong position).
The players play face to face, and the user copies each call and answer into the app. From this history, the app:
- calculates every number that is still possible and shows how many remain
- suggests the five best next calls, ranked by expected information (bits)
- tracks how far the opponent has narrowed down the user's number and shows who is closer to winning
- lists all remaining candidates, with a filter by leading digits
- detects contradictory entries and lets the user undo the last entry
Target audience: players of this game and people who enjoy logic puzzles. Tracking every possible number by hand is tedious and error-prone; the app does this and shows which calls give the most information.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login, account, credentials, or sample files are required. All features are available right after launch.
The user interface is in Japanese. English translations are given in brackets.
1. On first launch, a tutorial explains the rules and the app. Swipe through the pages or tap "スキップ" [Skip]. Reopen it anytime with the "?" button (top right).
2. Choose 3, 4, or 5 digits and tap "ゲームを始める" [Start game].
3. In the "自分の推理" [My deduction] tab, enter a call, select the number of EAT and BITE, and tap "履歴に追加" [Add to history]. The remaining candidates and "最善手" [Best moves] are updated. Tapping a best move fills it into the input field.
4. Switch to the "相手の推理" [Opponent's deduction] tab at the top to enter the opponent's calls and the user's answers.
5. Tap "すべて見る" [See all] to open the full list of remaining candidates.
6. If an entry contradicts earlier ones, the app shows a warning. Tap "最後の履歴を取り消す" [Undo last entry] to fix it.
7. Use "やり直す" [Restart] (top right) to start a new game.
